## 1. Project Philosophy & Core Purpose \[EXTRACT INTO SEPARATE FILE]

- Why Axon exists
  - All products I know are focused around agentic flows. I don't like it because they give insanely strong incentive to delegate thinking to the agent. So as Google made humanity forgetful - AI kills our curiosity and quality of thought, which not only affects one's projects-work, but also makes the whole life worse.
  - Axon exists to create an opposition. To use LLM as a proper tool instead of one's replacement, to enhance quality of thought, to not tempt by ease of delegation.

- ## Core problem it solves

- Key differentiators and product vision
  - What if we will have the super smart LLM that can do ideal code, have 100m context with like perfect reasoning throughout of it that is coupled with indistinguishable from human AI assistant that responsds in real time and remembers everything.
    1. LLM didn't make scientific breakthrough. Which means their creativity isn't even remotely comparable to some human brains. All their grandiose results are like one-shotting a game that already exists, rewriting compiler that already is written, and so on. Human will be directing LLMs for a long time.
    2. If human needs to guide LLM - human must have good quality of thought. If current tendency continues, where agents are used to delegate everything even shallow thinking/problem solving - quality of thought will drop significantly.
    3. That's why this project has potential for being useful for decades. It's not "agentic" thinking delegation. It's focused on making delegating "inconvenient". It keeps human in the loop WHILE providing all instruments to convieniet usage of LLM as a tool to imrpove one's quality of thought,which equals to quality of life. While it restricts pathological usage of LLM.
       3.1. Writing is thinking. When one reflects/brainstorm - he gets kinda random insights and extracts them "on paper" in a structured manner. Then he can use those structured insights to improve upon them. Separate the wheat from the chaff, focus on specific points, represent them with higher precision and more details. And this process can go on and on and on.
       3.1.5 You can't do that with speach to text. Period. You can't talk with LLM and have something complex and personally meaningful out of it. You must work on the written document. You must constantly tinker with the whole document at once. Because that's how brain works when it's "charged up" around information/structures/associations from/around that document.
       3.2. To sum up - LLMs will still need the creative input. LLMs still won't see the whole picture that is living in human's brain and they won't be able to "guess" it. And there won't be nothing worthwhile in that brain, if a human doesn't learn(remember) and think and practice all the time.
       3.3. So, current focus of the project on writing + having convenience of using LLM as a tool + restricting temptations - won't "get old" for decades at least. Because it doesn't matter if LLM can do all the work and thinking for you if you will live in idocracy.

- "Temptation-management decisions"
  - No automatic worktrees - meaning you won't be tempted to run lots of tasks in parallel which catastrophically reduces quality of review and the whole codebase because of it.
  - No auto-approval. Edgy thing, there may be smarter way to deal with it. But for now - having no auto-approval is miles better than having it ON or partially ON.
