## Features

- Notifications for meals. 30 minutes early, huge. Sets the mind into "time to wrap up" state, and makes skipping/delaying meal less likely. Which means weaker post-meal crashing out. Since it's easy to recover from 30% cognitive capacity than from like 5%, where you struggle to even go for a meal.
- Secondary tasks. Have a way to offload them somewhere and then process later.
  - Time
    - Like mornings should be 100% on the main task.
    - Nights? Yeah. Around meals too.
  - Prevention
    - I spend like an hour on trying to debug obsidian scroll issues. If I postponed this to like evening/night - I wouldn't even care to debug by that time, so it would've been 1 hour saved + lots of productive work saved by not spending peak energy time on this PS

## reasons to have schedule

### Plan-like execution tracking/feeling for continuous tasks

- Examples
  - sleep schedule
  - meals
  - possible exercising
  - cleaning
  - learning sessions
  - meditations
  - dream analysis sessions
- Without that - you're like tapping into the chaos. Meal for example? am I hungry enough? How long I worked? Should I go eating? Or just relax?

### Indulgence tracking

- I'm really shameful about that since started public thing. But I know I'll have to step over this obstacle, and bring this part of me to the public light as well.
- Like PMO is there. Pizza is there. They're not creating much issues, I'm staying productive. But they're there. And they may create issues later. That's why I need the schedule. I already have an example that was helpful. and made me aware to creeping in indulgence.
  - Like I checked weekly view and it was like, one PMO, then one PMO, then PMO twice, then PMO twice and a pizza, and it ended in like pmo + pizza + sweets + youtube binge loops. Without the schedule I would have like very rough notion about that. I won't be aware of it for sure, because how indulgence creates temporary amnesia of that moment (because I'm possessed by the complex).
- So for sure if you indulge, but honestly schedule that in - it helps a lot.
- And as a small bonus, it creates a bit additional resistance to indulge, because like you'll have to publicly set it in the schedule that is visible for the whole duration of recording.
