Hi, I’m Taras, 28, from Ukraine.
My life became a complete trainwreck, and I’m desperately trying to put it back together.

The main issue is that I’m extremely isolated, which leaves me without a sense of meaning in my life.

My current idea is to build a "sense of belonging" by sharing my life publicly and meaningfully through **the Framework**. Hopefully I'll be able to create a mutually beneficial community of like-minded people around it - one where we can help each other find meaning and sustain our efforts on the path of aiming up.

### The pieces

- **[Website](https://taras.life)**
  - The main entry point to the Framework, with the full context and explanations.
- **[Website repository](https://github.com/tarasHQ/taras-life)**
  - The website's source code. Based on [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/).
- **[Obsidian vault repository](https://github.com/tarasHQ/Obsidian-Vault)** _← you are here_
  - [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) vault. My notes, reflections, plans, and journal entries. Also includes the `.obsidian` folder with all the plugins, CSS snippets and settings.
  - Almost all of the vault is published as-is to preserve transparency. Only a few files and an unsorted junk folder remain private.
- **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@life-of-taras)**
  - YouTube channel where I record my life as I try to rebuild it.
  - The aim is to show a real person, flaws, struggles and bad angles included - without polishing the footage in post-production or condensing it into a perfect 15-minute “day in my life” video.
