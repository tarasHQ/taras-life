[[On Temptations]]

## Description

Any kind of sexually stimulating activity. Watching/listening/doing/fantasizing

- ### Issue #1 - Low likelihood of "brain reset"
  When brain's context is overloaded - PMO is not helping to reset it
  - ##### Discussion
    - ###### The issue in practice shows like <10% success rate. 90% chance to spend badly additional X hours.
      - September 11th, 12th, 13th, 14th - at best you get another 90 minutes of work and then further indulge anyway.
        - **Opposition** it isn't true. Without work it's obvious that it'll lead nowhere, everything will. With work though, you had Sep 13th, where 15 minutes PMO lead to another 90 minutes of work until _planned_ meal. Then another PMO 7 hours later when you were exhausted, you PMO'd, ate and work continues.
        - **Opposition** only proof you have is that when you did PMO when you also had sweets for the whole day. Then it became catalyst to the indulgence. But on practice, you have no proofs that it leads nowhere.
    - ###### You know for sure that there's additional tax/hit on the productivity for the rest of the day
      - Because you're seeking for more soothing since it's on the table. So you want to relax, sleep, lay in bed and watch YT.
- ### Issue #2 - The Loop
  High chance to move to either games or youtube after PMO, instead of productive work
  - ##### Discussion
    - ###### Behavioral loop that was conditioned for years
      - Even when my awareness was low - I was aware of that loop. Even when I have no access to gluttony - PMO very often pushes me into YouTube or games. And those then push me into gluttony or PMO again. So the loop is self-sustaining.
      - **Opposition** But why won't you do only PMO? Loops happens when everything is on the table and you have 0 breaks.
        - \[STRONG] - After PMO you're in state to put thing on the table. Youtube is like few minutes away from that. As well as ordering a pizza or downloading/playing a game. It's an illusion that those are not on the table, they're always are.
      - **Opposition** And PMO pushes you into youtube and games because you initially wanted that. If PMO happens around the work when you're off youtube/games and not having resistance to work - PMO doesn't intervene in that either.
        - \[STRONG] - But you see in relapses that it still made you want youtube/sweets/pizza. Like it's pretty strong craving. I might even succumb to it. ... Yeah, I did it. I wanted to resist, but I just did it. Partially to make a point, but mostly because it's a damn good justification to order a pizza. On top of that you ordered it after you had a pretty big "healthy" meal 3 hours ago. So it doesn't even make sense, I'm still feeling full and not hungry at all.
- ### Issue #3 - Uncertainty of the work
  When I'm struggle to move because I'm not sure what to do in my project
  - ##### Discussion
    - ###### It just doesn't help, although it promises help

## Insights

- ### Reducing harm
  - ##### Uncertainty trigger / any other trigger
    Run 15-30min timer. Get out from that state. Find what to do. THEN you can PMO. It works.
    PMO does not harm productivity only if it's not used as "invoked by the state-of-mind-behavior"
