### Rule #1 - Maximum strictness

- ##### Discussion
  - ###### I handle temptations on good days without problem. On bad days - it easily throws me off the track for days/weeks just by being caught in a single random bad event.
    - \[STRONG]"September 14th or 15th. Why not PMO, I'm happy, I'll work - then I'll be just fine" - result? PMO/YouTube/Pizza/Sweets for 95% of the time for the next 3 days. And I'm writing this in an absolute mess of a state that will have lots of friction to get back to at least half of productivity I need.
      - Again, it's not about good days. I know you can handle those. But it's just simple question of how poorly you handle bad days, where fait is little to none. Any slipped temptation will reduce importance of anything and it'll stop matter for you.
      - Don't play with fire. Don't play with Devil. There's no safety from the snake even in the Garden of Eden. As long as you can consider to give in or to not to give in - you're fucked.
- ##### Tips & Tricks
  - Ignore future. Ignore thinking about how long you need to live like that.
    - Approach the problem of temptation just one day at a time. You wake up, your goal is to keep it together **today**, that's it. Doesn't matter what about tomorrow. Or what about planning ordering sweets with other necessary things. There's only today, here and now.
